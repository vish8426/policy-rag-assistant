# Helix Technologies - Confidentiality & Intellectual Property Policy

**Document ID:** LEGAL-001
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Legal

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains employees' obligations to protect Helix's confidential information and
to assign work-related intellectual property to the Company. It supplements the
confidentiality and IP terms in each employee's offer and proprietary information agreement.

## 2. What Is Confidential Information

Confidential information includes any non-public information about Helix, its customers or
its partners, such as:

- Source code, system designs and technical documentation.
- Product roadmaps and unreleased features.
- Financial data, pricing and business plans.
- Customer lists and customer data.
- Employee personal data.

Confidential information may be in any form - documents, code, email, conversations or
verbal disclosures.

## 3. Protecting Confidential Information

- Access and use confidential information only as needed for your job.
- Store and share confidential information only through approved systems (see Information
  Security Policy, SEC-001 and the data classification levels described there).
- Do not disclose confidential information to anyone outside Helix without authorization and,
  where required, a signed non-disclosure agreement.
- Do not paste confidential information into unapproved third-party or AI tools (see Acceptable
  Use Policy, SEC-002).

## 4. Obligations After Employment Ends

- Confidentiality obligations **continue after employment ends**.
- On separation, return or delete all confidential information and company property following
  the Onboarding & Offboarding Policy (HR-005).
- Do not use Helix confidential information for the benefit of a future employer.

## 5. Ownership Of Work Product

- Work that employees create within the scope of their employment or using Company
  resources, **belongs to Helix**, including software, designs, documentation and inventions.
- Employees assign such intellectual property to Helix as set out in their proprietary
  information and inventions agreement, to the extent permitted by law.
- Employees agree to cooperate in documenting and registering Company intellectual property
  (for example, signing patent paperwork).

## 6. Prior And Personal Inventions

- Inventions an employee made before joining Helix, listed in their onboarding paperwork, are
  not assigned to Helix.
- Personal projects developed entirely on personal time, without Company resources and
  unrelated to Helix's business generally remain the employee's own, subject to applicable
  state law.

## 7. Third-Party And Open-Source Materials

- Respect third-party intellectual property. Do not copy code, content or trademarks without
  the right to use them.
- Use of open-source software must comply with its license and with Helix's open-source
  guidelines; check with Legal or the Security Team if unsure.

## 8. Use Of Company Trademarks

The Helix name, logo and brand assets may be used only in line with brand guidelines and
with Marketing's approval (see Social Media & External Communications, COMMS-001).

## 9. Reporting Concerns

Report any suspected leak or misuse of confidential information or intellectual property to
**legal@helixtech.example** and the Security Team (**security@helixtech.example**).

## 10. Contact

Questions about confidentiality and intellectual property should go to
**legal@helixtech.example**.
