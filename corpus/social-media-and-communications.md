# Helix Technologies - Social Media & External Communications Policy

**Document ID:** COMMS-001
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Marketing & Legal

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how employees should handle social media and other external
communications so that Helix's reputation and confidential information are protected. It
supports the Acceptable Use Policy (SEC-002) and Data Privacy Policy (SEC-003).

## 2. Personal Social Media

- Employees are free to use personal social media on their own time and devices.
- When discussing Helix, make clear that views are your own. A simple disclaimer such as
  "opinions are my own" is encouraged on profiles that identify your employer.
- Do **not** share confidential or restricted information (see SEC-001), unannounced
  products, customer names or internal financials.

## 3. Speaking On Behalf Of The Company

- Only authorized spokespeople may make official statements on behalf of Helix.
- Press or analyst inquiries must be forwarded to **press@helixtech.example**. Do not respond
  directly to journalists.
- Requests to speak at conferences or publish articles that reference Helix should be cleared
  with Marketing first.

## 4. Respectful Conduct Online

- The Code of Conduct (HR-006), including the prohibition on harassment and discrimination,
  applies to online interactions that involve colleagues or are connected to work.
- Do not post content that is unlawful, harassing or that discloses another employee's
  personal information.

## 5. Customer And Partner References

- Do not name customers or partners publicly without approval from Marketing and the
  customer, even in a positive context.
- Case studies and testimonials are coordinated by Marketing under signed agreements.

## 6. Intellectual Property And Endorsements

- Respect copyright and trademarks when posting images, logos or text.
- If you receive something of value in exchange for a post about a product, disclose it as
  required by applicable advertising rules.

## 7. Protecting The Brand

- Report impersonation accounts or leaked confidential information to Marketing and the
  Security Team (**security@helixtech.example**).
- The Helix logo and brand assets must be used according to the brand guidelines maintained by
  Marketing.

## 8. Contact

Questions about external communications should go to **marketing@helixtech.example**.
