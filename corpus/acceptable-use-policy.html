Helix Technologies - Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: SEC-002
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Security Team

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy describes how employees may use Helix systems, devices, networks and accounts. It supports the Information Security Policy (SEC-001).

2. Acceptable Use

Company systems are provided for business purposes. Reasonable, incidental personal use is permitted as long as it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources or violate any policy.

3. Prohibited Activities

  • Sharing accounts or credentials or attempting to access systems you are not authorized to use.
  • Installing unlicensed, pirated or unapproved software.
  • Storing company Confidential or Restricted data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts.
  • Using company systems to harass, discriminate or distribute unlawful content.
  • Disabling security controls such as encryption, endpoint protection or the VPN.
  • Conducting a personal business or political campaign using company resources.

4. Email And Messaging

Use company email and Slack professionally. Do not auto-forward company email to personal accounts. Be cautious with attachments and links and report suspected phishing to security@helixtech.example.

5. Use Of Generative AI Tools

Employees may use approved generative AI tools for productivity. Do not paste Confidential or Restricted company data, customer data or secrets into any AI tool that has not been approved by the Security Team. A list of approved tools is maintained on the security intranet page.

6. Monitoring

Helix may monitor and log use of company systems to the extent permitted by law, for security, compliance and operational purposes. Employees should not expect privacy in their use of company systems.

7. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices may be used to access email and Slack only if enrolled in the company mobile device management (MDM) profile, which enforces a passcode and allows remote wipe of company data.

8. Consequences

Violations may lead to revocation of access and disciplinary action up to and including termination.

9. Contact

Questions about acceptable use should go to security@helixtech.example.
