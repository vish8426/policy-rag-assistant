# Helix Technologies - Procurement & Vendor Management Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-003
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Helix purchases goods and services and how it manages vendors. It
ensures spending is controlled, vendors are vetted and contracts are properly reviewed. It
works alongside the Expense & Reimbursement Policy (FIN-001).

## 2. When This Policy Applies

This policy applies to **new purchases of goods or services from third parties**, including
software subscriptions, professional services and hardware bought at scale. Routine
reimbursable expenses are handled under FIN-001.

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Annual contract value | Required approval |
|-----------------------|-------------------|
| Up to $1,000 | Direct manager |
| $1,001 - $10,000 | Department director |
| $10,001 - $50,000 | VP of Finance |
| Over $50,000 | CFO and Legal |

These thresholds are based on the **total annual value** of the contract, not a single
invoice.

## 4. Vendor Selection

- For purchases over **$10,000 per year**, obtain at least **two competitive quotes** where
  practical.
- Prefer vendors that meet Helix's security and privacy requirements (see SEC-001, SEC-003).
- New software vendors that will handle company or customer data must complete a **security
  and privacy review** before contracts are signed.

## 5. Contracts

- All contracts must be reviewed by **Legal** before signature. Only authorized signatories
  may sign contracts on behalf of Helix; individual employees may not.
- Auto-renewing contracts must be tracked so renewals are reviewed before they take effect.

## 6. Purchase Process

1. Submit a purchase request in the procurement system with a business justification.
2. Obtain the approval required for the contract value (see Section 3).
3. Legal and, where applicable, Security review the contract.
4. Finance issues a purchase order; vendors should not begin work before a PO is issued.

## 7. Data Processing Agreements

Vendors that process personal data on behalf of Helix must sign a data processing agreement
before any data is shared, consistent with the Data Privacy Policy (SEC-003).

## 8. Vendor Performance And Offboarding

- Significant vendors are reviewed annually for performance, security posture and cost.
- When a vendor relationship ends, IT and Security ensure access is revoked and any Helix data
  is returned or securely deleted.

Questions about procurement should go to **finance@helixtech.example**.
