# Helix Technologies - Travel Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-002
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs business travel booked and paid for by Helix. It works together with the
Expense & Reimbursement Policy (FIN-001).

## 2. Booking

- Book all business travel through the approved travel tool, **TravelHub**.
- Book airfare and hotels at least **14 days in advance** where possible to control costs.
- Travel that cannot be booked through TravelHub must be approved in advance by a department
  director and submitted with receipts.

## 3. Air Travel

- **Economy class** is the standard for all flights with a scheduled duration of **6 hours or
  less**.
- **Premium economy or business class** may be booked for flights with a scheduled duration
  of **more than 6 hours**, with director approval.
- **First class is not reimbursable** under any circumstances.
- Employees keep frequent-flyer miles earned on business travel.

## 4. Lodging

- Hotels should be booked at a **standard business rate, with a cap of $250 per night** in
  most U.S. cities. Higher-cost cities (for example, New York and San Francisco) have a cap
  of **$350 per night**.
- Incidental room charges such as movies and minibar are not reimbursable.

## 5. Ground Transportation

- Use ride-share, taxis, public transit or rental cars as appropriate.
- **Rental cars should be mid-size or smaller** unless three or more employees are traveling
  together.
- Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.

## 6. Meals While Traveling

Meals are reimbursed under the per-diem rule in the Expense Policy (FIN-001): **$75 per day
domestic** and **$100 per day international**.

## 7. International Travel

- International travel requires **VP approval** before booking.
- Helix reimburses the cost of required visas, vaccinations and travel insurance for
  business trips abroad.

## 8. Safety

Employees should register international itineraries with People Operations. In an emergency
while traveling, contact the 24/7 support line listed in TravelHub.

## 9. Contact

Questions about travel should go to **finance@helixtech.example**.
