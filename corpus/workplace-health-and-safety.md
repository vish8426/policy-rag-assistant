# Helix Technologies - Workplace Health, Safety & Ergonomics Policy

**Document ID:** HR-009
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations & Facilities

## 1. Purpose

Helix is committed to the health and safety of every employee, whether working from home, at
the Austin hub or while traveling. This policy describes safety expectations and the support
Helix provides.

## 2. Remote Workspace Safety

- Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe home workspace: stable furniture, clear
  walkways and working smoke detectors in the home.
- Helix provides a **one-time home office setup stipend of $750** (see Remote Work Policy,
  HR-003) that may be used for ergonomic furniture such as an adjustable chair or
  sit-stand desk.
- Employees may request an **ergonomic assessment** through People Operations; assessments
  are conducted virtually at no cost.

## 3. Hub Office Safety

- The Austin hub office complies with applicable OSHA requirements.
- Visitors must sign in at reception and be escorted by an employee.
- Emergency exits, fire extinguishers and first-aid kits are clearly marked. Evacuation
  routes are posted on each floor.

## 4. Reporting Injuries

- Report any work-related injury or illness - including injuries that occur while working
  from home - to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example** within **24 hours**.
- Helix maintains workers' compensation insurance as required by law. People Operations helps
  employees file claims.

## 5. Ergonomics Guidance

- Position the top of the monitor at or slightly below eye level, about an arm's length away.
- Keep wrists neutral and feet flat on the floor or a footrest.
- Take a short break to stand and move at least once per hour.

## 6. Mental Health And Wellbeing

- The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free, confidential counseling (see Benefits &
  Compensation, HR-004).
- The **$50 per month wellness reimbursement** may be used for mental-health apps and
  services.
- Managers are encouraged to support reasonable flexibility for employees managing health
  needs.

## 7. Illness And Contagious Conditions

- Employees who are unwell should use **sick leave** (see PTO & Leave, HR-002) and not work
  while contagious.
- Helix may adjust office attendance guidance during public-health events based on official
  guidance.

## 8. Emergencies

- For any life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the U.S.)
  first.
- Then notify People Operations and, if at the hub, building security.

## 9. Contact

Questions about health and safety should go to People Operations at
**people@helixtech.example**.
