# Helix Technologies - Grievance & Dispute Resolution Policy

**Document ID:** HR-011
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Purpose

Helix wants every employee to feel heard and treated fairly. This policy describes how to
raise a workplace concern or grievance and how Helix responds. It works alongside the Code of
Conduct (HR-006).

## 2. What This Policy Covers

This policy covers concerns about working conditions, the application of Company policies,
interpersonal conflicts and unfair treatment. Concerns about harassment, discrimination or
ethics violations may also be raised through the channels in the Code of Conduct (HR-006),
including the confidential ethics hotline at **1-800-555-0142**.

## 3. Step 1 - Informal Resolution

- Where it is safe and appropriate, first raise the concern directly with the person involved
  or with your manager.
- Many concerns are resolved quickly through an open conversation. People Operations can help
  prepare for or facilitate that conversation.

## 4. Step 2 - Formal Grievance

If an informal approach does not resolve the issue or is not appropriate:

- Submit a written grievance to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example**, describing
  the concern, the people involved and the outcome you are seeking.
- People Operations acknowledges receipt within **3 business days**.

## 5. Step 3 - Review And Investigation

- People Operations reviews the grievance and may interview those involved and any witnesses.
- Investigations are handled **confidentially** to the extent possible and are conducted
  impartially.
- Most investigations are completed within **20 business days**. If more time is needed, the
  employee is kept informed.

## 6. Step 4 - Outcome And Appeal

- The employee receives a written summary of the outcome and any actions to be taken.
- If the employee is not satisfied, they may **appeal in writing within 10 business days** to
  the VP of People, whose decision is final.

## 7. Protection From Retaliation

Helix prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a grievance in good faith or who
participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of the Code of Conduct
(HR-006) and should be reported immediately.

## 8. Confidentiality

Information about a grievance is shared only with those who need to know to investigate and
resolve it. Employees involved in a grievance are expected to respect the confidentiality of
the process.

## 9. Records

People Operations keeps records of formal grievances and their outcomes in line with the
employee-records retention period in the Data Privacy Policy (SEC-003).

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy should go to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example**.
