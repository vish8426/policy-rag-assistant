# Helix Technologies - Onboarding & Offboarding Policy

**Document ID:** HR-005
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations & IT

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the standard process for bringing new employees on board and for
separating employees from the Company in an orderly, secure way.

## 2. Before The First Day

- People Operations sends an offer packet and collects signed documents, including the I-9
  and tax forms.
- IT ships a **company laptop** and provisions accounts at least **three business days**
  before the start date.
- The hiring manager prepares a 30/60/90-day onboarding plan.

## 3. First Week

- New employees complete required compliance training, including **Security Awareness
  training (see SEC-001)** within their **first 7 days**.
- Employees set up multi-factor authentication on all company accounts on day one.
- People Operations runs a benefits enrollment session.

## 4. Probationary Period

The first **90 days** of employment are an introductory period during which the manager
provides structured feedback. Successful completion does not change the at-will nature of
employment.

## 5. Offboarding - Voluntary Resignation

- Employees are asked to give at least **two weeks' notice** of resignation.
- The manager notifies People Operations and IT immediately upon receiving notice.
- People Operations schedules an exit interview.

## 6. Offboarding - Final Day Checklist

On or before an employee's final day:

- **All company hardware** (laptop, peripherals, access cards) must be returned. IT provides
  a prepaid shipping label for remote employees.
- IT **disables all system access at the end of the final working day**.
- Finance processes the final paycheck, including any payout of accrued PTO required by state
  law (see HR-002).
- People Operations provides information about benefits continuation (COBRA) and final
  expense submission, which must be filed within **30 days** of the last day.

## 7. Return Of Equipment

Employees who do not return company equipment within **14 days** of their last day may have
the fair market value of the equipment deducted from final pay where permitted by law and
may be billed for the remainder.

## 8. Contact

Questions about onboarding or offboarding should go to People Operations at
**people@helixtech.example** and IT at **it@helixtech.example**.
