# Helix Technologies - Data Privacy Policy

**Document ID:** SEC-003
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Legal & Security

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Helix handles personal data belonging to customers, employees and
website visitors and the obligations employees have when working with that data. It supports
compliance with applicable privacy laws, including the **GDPR** and the **CCPA/CPRA**.

## 2. Principles

Helix processes personal data according to these principles:

- **Lawfulness and transparency.** We process data only for legitimate, disclosed purposes.
- **Data minimization.** We collect only the data we need.
- **Purpose limitation.** Data collected for one purpose is not reused for an incompatible
  purpose.
- **Accuracy and retention.** We keep data accurate and only as long as necessary.
- **Security.** Personal data is protected per the Information Security Policy (SEC-001).

## 3. Categories Of Personal Data

- **Customer data** processed on behalf of customers under our Data Processing Agreement.
- **Employee data** collected for employment and benefits administration.
- **Website data** such as analytics and contact-form submissions.

## 4. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Customer account data | Duration of contract plus 90 days |
| Job applicant data (not hired) | 1 year |
| Employee records | 7 years after employment ends |
| Website analytics | 26 months |

Data is deleted or anonymized at the end of its retention period unless a legal hold applies.

## 5. Data Subject Requests (DSARs)

- Individuals may request access to, correction of or deletion of their personal data.
- Requests are submitted to **privacy@helixtech.example**.
- Helix responds to verified requests within **30 days**, extendable as permitted by law.
- Employees who receive a privacy request from a customer or individual must forward it to
  the privacy mailbox the same business day and must not respond directly.

## 6. Data Breaches

- Any actual or suspected breach of personal data must be reported to
  **security@helixtech.example immediately**.
- The Security and Legal teams assess the breach and handle any required notification to
  regulators and affected individuals within the timelines required by law (for example,
  **72 hours** for certain GDPR notifications).

## 7. Vendors And Data Transfers

- Vendors that process personal data on our behalf must sign a data processing agreement.
- International data transfers use approved safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. Employee Responsibilities

- Access personal data only when needed for your job.
- Never copy customer personal data to personal devices or unapproved tools (see SEC-002).
- Complete annual privacy training.

## 9. Contact

Questions about privacy should go to **privacy@helixtech.example**.
