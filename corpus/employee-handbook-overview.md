# Helix Technologies - Employee Handbook Overview

**Document ID:** HR-001
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Purpose

This handbook introduces the policies and procedures that govern employment at Helix
Technologies, Inc. ("Helix," "the Company"). It applies to all full-time and part-time
employees in the United States. Contractors and interns are covered only where a policy
explicitly says so. Where a specific policy document conflicts with this overview, the
specific policy document controls.

## 2. About Helix

Helix Technologies is a remote-first software company headquartered in Austin, Texas. We
build data analytics tooling for mid-market companies. The Company was founded in 2018 and
employs roughly 320 people across the United States.

## 3. Our Values

- **Customer obsession.** We start from the customer's problem and work backward.
- **Default to transparency.** Information is shared openly unless there is a clear reason
  not to.
- **Bias for action.** We prefer shipping and learning over waiting for certainty.
- **Respect.** We assume good intent and treat one another with professionalism.

## 4. Employment Basics

- **Employment at will.** Employment with Helix is at will. Either the employee or the
  Company may end the relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice, subject to
  applicable law.
- **Standard work week.** Full-time is 40 hours per week. Helix operates on a flexible
  schedule with **core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in each employee's
  local time zone**, during which employees are expected to be reachable.
- **Pay schedule.** Employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last business day
  of each month.
- **Classification.** Employees are classified as exempt or non-exempt under the Fair Labor
  Standards Act. Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime as required by law.
