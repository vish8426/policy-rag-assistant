# Helix Technologies - Benefits & Compensation Policy

**Document ID:** HR-004
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations & Total Rewards

## 1. Eligibility

Regular full-time employees (30+ hours per week) are eligible for the full benefits package.
Benefits begin on the **first day of the month following the date of hire**. Part-time
employees may be eligible for a subset of benefits as described in their offer.

## 2. Health Insurance

- Helix offers **medical, dental and vision** coverage through Blue Shield.
- Helix pays **90% of the premium for employees** and **70% for dependents**.
- Coverage elections are made during onboarding and during the annual open enrollment period
  each November. Outside those windows, changes require a qualifying life event (marriage,
  birth, loss of other coverage) reported within **30 days**.

## 3. Retirement - 401(k)

- Employees may contribute to a **401(k)** plan administered by Fidelity beginning on their
  first day.
- Helix matches **100% of the first 4%** of eligible compensation that an employee
  contributes.
- Employer matching contributions **vest immediately**.

## 4. Equity

Most full-time employees receive equity in the form of restricted stock units (RSUs) as
part of their offer. RSUs vest over **four years with a one-year cliff**. Details are
provided in each employee's individual grant agreement.

## 5. Learning & Development

- Each employee receives an **annual learning & development budget of $1,500** for courses,
  books, conferences and certifications relevant to their role.
- L&D spending is reimbursed through the expense system (FIN-001) and requires manager
  approval before purchase.
- Unused L&D budget does **not** carry over to the next year.

## 6. Wellness

- Helix provides a **$50 per month wellness reimbursement** for gym memberships, fitness
  apps or mental-health services.
- All employees have access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), which offers free,
  confidential counseling sessions.

## 7. Other Benefits

- **Life and disability insurance.** Helix provides basic life insurance equal to 1x annual
  salary, plus short- and long-term disability coverage, at no cost to the employee.
- **Commuter benefits.** Employees who work from the Austin hub may elect pre-tax commuter
  benefits.
- **Paid parental leave.** See the PTO & Leave Policy (HR-002).

## 8. Compensation Reviews

Compensation is reviewed annually during the spring review cycle. Promotions and market
adjustments may occur off-cycle with director approval.

## 9. Contact

Questions about benefits should go to **benefits@helixtech.example**.
