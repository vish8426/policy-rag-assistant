# Helix Technologies - Code Of Conduct

**Document ID:** HR-006
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations & Legal

## 1. Our Commitment

Helix is committed to a respectful, inclusive and safe workplace. This Code of Conduct
applies to all employees, contractors and anyone acting on behalf of the Company, in all
work settings including remote, travel and company events.

## 2. Professional Behavior

- Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate honestly and constructively.
- Maintain a workplace free from disruptive, threatening or violent behavior.

## 3. Anti-Harassment And Anti-Discrimination

- Helix prohibits discrimination and harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual
  orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status or any
  other protected characteristic.
- Sexual harassment of any kind is prohibited, including unwelcome advances and the creation
  of a hostile work environment.
- This standard applies to all communication channels, including Slack, email and video.

## 4. Equal Opportunity

Employment decisions are based on merit, qualifications and business needs. Helix is an
equal-opportunity employer and provides reasonable accommodation for disabilities and
religious practices.

## 5. Conflicts Of Interest

- Avoid situations where personal interests conflict with the Company's interests.
- Disclose outside employment, board positions or financial interests in competitors,
  customers or vendors to People Operations.
- Employees may not hire or directly manage a family member or romantic partner without
  disclosure and an approved management plan.

## 6. Gifts And Anti-Bribery

- Do not offer or accept gifts, entertainment or payments intended to improperly influence a
  business decision.
- Gifts of nominal value (under **$100**) may be accepted but should be disclosed if they
  could appear to create a conflict.
- Helix prohibits bribery and complies with the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act.

## 7. Confidentiality And Company Property

- Protect confidential and proprietary information during and after employment.
- Use company property and funds responsibly and only for legitimate business purposes.

## 8. Reporting Concerns

- Report suspected violations to your manager, to People Operations
  (**people@helixtech.example**) or through the **confidential ethics hotline at
  1-800-555-0142**, which allows anonymous reports.
- Helix prohibits **retaliation** against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or
  participates in an investigation.

## 9. Investigations And Consequences

Helix investigates reported violations promptly and confidentially to the extent possible.
Substantiated violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this Code should go to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example**.
