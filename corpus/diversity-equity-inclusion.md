# Helix Technologies - Diversity, Equity & Inclusion Policy

**Document ID:** HR-010
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Our Commitment

Helix is committed to building a diverse workforce and an inclusive culture where every
employee can do their best work. This policy describes our commitments and the programs that
support them. It complements the Code of Conduct (HR-006).

## 2. Equal Opportunity

Helix is an equal-opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color,
religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran
status, genetic information or any other characteristic protected by law. This applies to
hiring, promotion, compensation, training and all other terms of employment.

## 3. Inclusive Hiring

- Job descriptions are written to be inclusive and focus on the skills required for the role.
- Interview panels are encouraged to include diverse perspectives.
- Helix uses structured interviews and consistent evaluation criteria to reduce bias.

## 4. Reasonable Accommodation

- Helix provides reasonable accommodation for employees and applicants with disabilities and
  for sincerely held religious practices.
- Requests for accommodation are made to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example**
  and are handled confidentially through an interactive process.
- The **2 floating holidays** each year (see Company Holidays, HR-007) may be used for
  religious or cultural observances.

## 5. Employee Resource Groups

- Helix supports employee resource groups (ERGs) that bring together employees with shared
  backgrounds or interests and their allies.
- ERGs may request a modest annual budget for events through People Operations.

## 6. Pay Equity

- Helix reviews compensation periodically to identify and address unexplained pay gaps across
  demographic groups.
- Compensation is based on role, level, location and performance, as described in the
  Benefits & Compensation Policy (HR-004).

## 7. Inclusive Everyday Practices

- Schedule meetings within core collaboration hours (10:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m. local time) where
  possible, mindful of time zones, so remote employees can participate.
- Use inclusive language and respect colleagues' stated pronouns.
- Provide captions for recorded company presentations where feasible.

## 8. Reporting Concerns

Concerns about discrimination or exclusion can be raised with a manager, People Operations,
or the confidential ethics hotline at **1-800-555-0142** (see HR-006). Helix prohibits
retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## 9. Contact

Questions about diversity, equity and inclusion should go to **people@helixtech.example**.
