# Helix Technologies - Expense & Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** FIN-001
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Finance

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Helix reimburses, the approval required and how
to submit claims. The Company reimburses reasonable, necessary expenses incurred in the
course of business.

## 2. General Rules

- Expenses must be **business-related, reasonable and supported by documentation**.
- A **receipt is required for any expense of $25 or more**. For expenses under $25, a
  description in the expense system is sufficient.
- Submit expenses within **30 days** of incurring them. Expenses submitted more than **60
  days** after they are incurred may be denied.
- Reimbursements are paid through payroll within **two pay cycles** of approval.

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Expense amount | Required approval |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Up to $500 | Direct manager |
| $501 - $5,000 | Department director |
| Over $5,000 | VP of Finance |

## 4. Meals And Entertainment

- **Per diem for meals while traveling is $75 per day** for domestic travel and $100 per day
  for international travel.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only for approved client entertainment, not for individual meals.
- Team meals and client entertainment require itemized receipts and a list of attendees.

## 5. Travel Expenses

Airfare, lodging and ground transportation are governed by the Travel Policy (FIN-002).
Travel booked outside the approved travel tool requires director approval.

## 6. Home Office And Equipment

- The one-time home office setup stipend and annual remote work stipend are described in the
  Remote Work Policy (HR-003) and do not require itemized receipts.
- Additional equipment purchases must be pre-approved by the employee's manager.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **not** reimbursable:

- Personal travel, personal entertainment and personal subscriptions.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- First-class airfare (see Travel Policy for cabin rules).
- Expenses without adequate documentation.

## 8. How To Submit

1. Log in to the expense system (Expensify).
2. Create a report, attach receipts and categorize each line item.
3. Submit to your manager for approval.
4. Approved reports are routed to Finance for payment.

## 9. Contact

Questions about expenses should go to **finance@helixtech.example**.
