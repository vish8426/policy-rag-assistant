# Helix Technologies - IT Support & Equipment Policy

**Document ID:** IT-001
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** IT Operations

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the technology equipment Helix provides, how to get IT support and
employees' responsibilities for company hardware and software. It works alongside the
Information Security Policy (SEC-001) and Acceptable Use Policy (SEC-002).

## 2. Standard Equipment

Every full-time employee receives:

- A **company laptop** (MacBook Pro or a Dell Latitude, based on role and preference).
- A standard accessory bundle: external keyboard, mouse and a 27-inch monitor on request.
- Software licenses required for the role, provisioned through single sign-on.

Specialized hardware (for example, additional monitors, high-memory workstations for data
engineering) may be requested through the IT portal with manager approval.

## 3. Requesting Support

- Submit all IT requests and issues through the **IT help desk portal** or by emailing
  **it@helixtech.example**.
- Service targets: **urgent issues** (cannot work) are acknowledged within **2 business
  hours**; **standard requests** are acknowledged within **1 business day**.
- For urgent security issues, contact the Security Team directly at
  **security@helixtech.example** (see SEC-001).

## 4. Equipment Refresh

- Laptops are refreshed on a **3-year cycle**. Employees may request an earlier replacement
  if hardware fails or no longer meets role requirements.
- Replaced equipment must be returned to IT for secure data wiping and recycling.

## 5. Software And Licenses

- Use of company-funded software requires it to be approved and provisioned by IT.
- Employees must not purchase software on a personal card and expense it without prior IT
  review, to avoid security and licensing issues.
- Requests for new SaaS tools go through IT and the Security Team for review.

## 6. Lost, Stolen Or Damaged Equipment

- Report lost or stolen devices to IT **and** the Security Team **immediately** so the device
  can be remotely locked or wiped.
- Accidental damage is covered by the Company. Repeated negligence may result in the employee
  bearing repair costs where permitted by law.

## 7. Personal Use And Data

- Limited personal use of company devices is permitted under the Acceptable Use Policy
  (SEC-002).
- Employees should not store important personal files on company devices, because devices may
  be wiped or reclaimed at any time, including at offboarding (see HR-005).

## 8. Return Of Equipment

All company equipment must be returned at the end of employment following the Onboarding &
Offboarding Policy (HR-005). IT provides a prepaid shipping label for remote employees.

## 9. Contact

Reach IT Operations at **it@helixtech.example**.
