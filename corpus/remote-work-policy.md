# Helix Technologies - Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** HR-003
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Overview

Helix is a remote-first company. Most roles may be performed remotely from anywhere within
the United States. This policy describes expectations, eligibility and the support Helix
provides for remote work.

## 2. Eligibility

- All employees are eligible to work remotely from their first day, subject to role
  requirements.
- A small number of roles (for example, certain IT and facilities positions) are designated
  on-site or hybrid; these are identified in the job description.
- Employees must work from a location within the United States. Working from outside the
  U.S. for more than **15 consecutive days** requires advance approval from People Operations
  due to tax and compliance implications.

## 3. Working Hours And Availability

- Employees are expected to be reachable during **core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to
  3:00 p.m. in their local time zone**.
- Outside core hours, employees may arrange their schedule to fit their responsibilities,
  provided deliverables and meetings are met.
- Employees must keep their calendar and status up to date in Slack and the company calendar.

## 4. Home Office Stipend And Equipment

- Helix provides a **company laptop** and required software to every employee.
- New employees receive a **one-time home office setup stipend of $750** for a desk, chair,
  or other ergonomic equipment.
- Employees receive an **annual remote work stipend of $500** to cover internet and ongoing
  home office costs. The annual stipend is paid each January.
- Equipment purchased with the setup stipend remains the property of the employee. Laptops
  and other IT-issued hardware remain the property of Helix and must be returned on
  separation (see Onboarding & Offboarding, HR-005).

## 5. Security Expectations

Remote workers must follow the Information Security Policy (SEC-001) and Acceptable Use
Policy (SEC-002), including full-disk encryption, multi-factor authentication and use of
the company VPN when accessing internal systems over untrusted networks.

## 6. Co-Working And Office Access

Employees who prefer to work outside the home may expense a co-working membership up to
**$300 per month** with manager approval. Helix maintains a hub office in Austin, Texas that
any employee may use.

## 7. Equipment Reimbursement And Home Visits

Helix does not conduct in-home inspections. Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe
and professional remote work environment.

## 8. Contact

Questions about remote work should go to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example**.
