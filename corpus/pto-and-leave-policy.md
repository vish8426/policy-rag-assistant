# Helix Technologies - PTO & Leave Policy

**Document ID:** HR-002
**Version:** 2026.1
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People Operations

## 1. Scope

This policy covers paid time off (PTO) and other leaves of absence for regular full-time
and part-time employees. Part-time employees accrue benefits on a pro-rated basis according
to scheduled hours.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

- **Annual allowance.** Full-time employees accrue **20 days (160 hours) of PTO per year**.
- **Accrual.** PTO accrues monthly at a rate of **1.67 days (13.33 hours) per month**.
  Accrual begins on the employee's first day of employment.
- **Carryover.** Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days** into the next
  calendar year. PTO above the carryover cap is forfeited on December 31 and is not paid out.
- **Payout at termination.** Accrued but unused PTO is paid out at termination where required
  by state law.
- **Requesting PTO.** Submit PTO requests in the HR system (BambooHR) at least **two weeks
  in advance** for planned time off. Requests are approved by the employee's manager subject
  to team coverage.

## 3. Sick Leave

- Sick leave is **separate from PTO**. Full-time employees receive **10 days (80 hours) of
  paid sick leave per year**, available from the first day of employment.
- Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness or to care for an immediate family
  member.
- Unused sick leave does **not** carry over and is **not** paid out at termination.
- For absences of **more than three consecutive days**, the Company may request a
  health-care provider's note.

## 4. Company Holidays

Helix observes 11 paid company holidays each year. The current year's dates are listed in
the Company Holidays document (HR-007). Holidays that fall on a weekend are observed on the
nearest weekday.

## 5. Parental Leave

- Eligible employees (those employed for at least **90 days**) receive **12 weeks of fully
  paid parental leave** following the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child.
- Parental leave must be taken within **12 months** of the qualifying event.
- Parental leave runs concurrently with leave under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)
  where applicable.

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to **5 paid days** of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate
family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent or
grandchild) and up to **1 paid day** for other relatives.

## 7. Jury Duty And Civic Leave

Helix provides paid leave for jury duty and for time required to vote. Provide a copy of the
jury summons to People Operations as soon as it is received.

## 8. Unpaid Leave

Requests for unpaid personal leave are considered case by case and require manager and
People Operations approval. Benefits continuation during unpaid leave follows the terms of
each benefit plan.

## 9. Contact

Questions about leave should go to People Operations at **people@helixtech.example**.
